I got a recruiter message last week that felt off.

The salary was unrealistic. The company name was vague. The email domain didn't match.
Classic signs — but easy to miss when you're deep in a job search and hopeful.

So I built something to catch it automatically.

Introducing Metier 🔍 (pronounced "met-yay").

From the word métier — your calling, your craft, the work you're meant to do. Metier helps
career professionals find the real thing and screen out the noise.

Paste any recruiter email, LinkedIn DM, or outreach message and get:

→ A Legitimacy Score (0–100), color-coded
→ LinkedIn identity check — does their background match who they claim to be?
→ Domain age and DNS verification — automatically
→ Live web research for scam reports tied to their domain
→ Tailored questions that smoke out ghost jobs without tipping them off
→ A job tracker for every role you're pursuing, with a legitimacy score baked in

The market is rough. Ghost jobs, fake recruiters, stolen identities. Metier puts the
intelligence back in your hands so you can move with confidence.

Met a recruiter? Run them through Metier first.

Powered by Claude AI. Takes 5 minutes to set up. Free to self-host.

A few things it caught in testing:
• A "Director of Engineering" role paying $600k with no interview process
• A recruiter whose email domain was registered 11 days before they reached out
• A message claiming to be from a Fortune 500 — sent from a Gmail address
• An "executive search director" whose LinkedIn showed Open to Work in an unrelated field

If you're job searching right now, run the last recruiter message you received through it.
